# Vela identity

Deep olive `#1d211c`, warm ivory `#faf9f2`, champagne `#978365`, muted stone `#d8d0bf`. Champagne serves as an accent; dark olive carries readable text and controls.

The palette was researched against the [Radix olive and gold scales](https://github.com/radix-ui/colors/blob/main/src/light.ts). Its restrained green and warm metallic neutrals complement wood, stone and linen interiors.

[Manrope](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope) provides clear navigation and interface text. [Cormorant Garamond](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond) provides expressive editorial headings. Variable font files are self-hosted under `public/fonts`; their SIL Open Font Licenses are in `brand/licenses`.

The architectural V logo and landing interior illustration were generated for Vela. The hero illustrates an interior mood, not the editor's rendering output. `scripts/brand.mjs` generates favicons, app icons, header marks and the social sharing image from `vela-logo-original.png`.

The landing-page bedroom and kitchen inspiration images are also AI-generated editorial imagery, not customer projects or output from Vela's renderer. The testimonial section uses explicitly labelled sample quotes until genuine approved customer feedback is supplied.
